How to Play

Movement

WASD or Arrow Keys — Move your champion

Combat

1 2 3 4 — Use abilities

Space — Basic attack (auto-attack when in range)

Objective

Defeat your opponent in 1v1 arena combat. First to reduce enemy health to zero wins the round. Best of 3 rounds wins the match!

Classes

Each class has unique abilities and playstyles. Warriors charge into melee, Mages kite with spells, Rogues burst from stealth, and Priests balance damage with healing.
